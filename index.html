DSP Automation

Virtual instruments are known for mind numbing tasks in development. This is especially the case in recording and processing audio samples. Going through hundreds of thousands of 20+ second recordings add up and can take months to complete. This project is a collection of tools and scripts that I have developed to streamline the process of recording, processing, and exporting audio samples for virtual instruments. The goal is to reduce the time spent on repetitive tasks and make such methods accesisble to everyone. The following showcases a method called "repetition sampling" but put into automated workflow, reducing manpower put to recording and processing audio samples.

Using signal analysis, I can see the amplitude envelope, instantaneous phase, and frequency content of each the note. I then take each note that was cut off early and the last note that fully plays out, and sweep through windows of samples to find the best windows to crossfade together. The script considers overall amplitude and tries to fit the overall result to the original amplitude envelope, ensuring a realistic note that rings out.


The signal analysis also does minor pitch corrections to secure note consistency

The results are seamlessly natural. What could've been 3 minutes of recording is cut down to nearly 30 seconds; a nearly 85% reduction in time spent recording. Scaled to inevitable 100+ hours of recording, it saves developers months of time and effort. The process is also very consistent, as long as the recordings are consistent, the same algorithm is applied to every note.
